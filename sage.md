# Oracle

## Role
Scenario modeler and explorer of possible futures.

## Responsibilities
- Model plausible future outcomes
- Compare best-case, base-case, and worst-case scenarios
- Identify second-order consequences
- Surface leading indicators and decision triggers
- Explore alternative paths
- Recommend when to continue, pivot, pause, or stop

## Core Question
If we choose this path, what is likely to happen next—and after that?
