# MavericksATeam

An AI-guided operating system for strategy, execution, research, building, critique, learning, and scenario planning.

## Mission

Turn complex goals into clear decisions, coordinated action, durable systems, and accumulated wisdom.

## The Team

| Agent | Primary Role |
|---|---|
| Chat | General strategist, facilitator, and decision integrator |
| Jeebs | Operations manager and project coordinator |
| Scout | Researcher and information gatherer |
| Forge | Builder of code, documents, systems, and deliverables |
| Sentinel | Critic who finds flaws, risks, assumptions, and blind spots |
| Sage | Synthesizer of principles, lessons, and long-term wisdom |
| Oracle | Scenario modeler who explores possible futures and second-order effects |

## The Operating Loop

```text
JEEBS
Intake, organize, assign, track
        |
        v
CHAT
Clarify the objective and integrate decisions
        |
        v
SCOUT
Gather facts, evidence, and context
        |
        v
FORGE
Build the artifact, system, or solution
        |
        v
SENTINEL
Stress-test the work
        |
        v
SAGE
Extract lessons and durable principles
        |
        v
JEEBS
Record decisions, assign next actions, update status
        |
        v
ORACLE
Model likely futures, alternatives, and second-order effects
        |
        +----> back to CHAT or JEEBS as needed
```

Jeebs sits at both the beginning and the end of each operating cycle: first to organize the work, and later to close the loop.

## Repository Map

- `agents/` — role charters and behavioral instructions
- `projects/` — active project workspaces
- `decisions/` — decision records
- `knowledge/` — durable facts, principles, and reference material
- `workflows/` — repeatable operating procedures
- `templates/` — reusable project, decision, research, and review templates
- `inbox/` — new ideas and unsorted requests
- `archive/` — completed or inactive work

## Start Here

1. Put a new request in `inbox/`.
2. Jeebs creates or updates a project folder.
3. Chat writes the project brief and decision question.
4. Scout researches.
5. Forge builds.
6. Sentinel reviews.
7. Sage records lessons.
8. Jeebs updates tasks and status.
9. Oracle models what happens next.

## Working Principle

Every meaningful project should leave behind:

- a clear decision,
- an owned next action,
- a reusable artifact,
- a recorded lesson,
- and an improved view of the future.
