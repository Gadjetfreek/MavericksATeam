# Standard Project Loop

## 1. Intake — Jeebs
Capture the request, desired outcome, constraints, owner, urgency, and definition of done.

## 2. Strategy — Chat
Clarify the true objective, decision question, priorities, and tradeoffs.

## 3. Research — Scout
Gather facts, evidence, source material, benchmarks, and unresolved questions.

## 4. Build — Forge
Create the proposed solution, artifact, system, code, or document.

## 5. Review — Sentinel
Stress-test assumptions, risks, quality, edge cases, and unintended effects.

## 6. Synthesis — Sage
Record lessons, reusable principles, and implications for other projects.

## 7. Closeout — Jeebs
Update status, decisions, owners, deadlines, blockers, and next actions.

## 8. Foresight — Oracle
Model future scenarios, identify trigger points, and recommend the next cycle.
